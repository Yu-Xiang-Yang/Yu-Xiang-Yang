## About

Researcher at Zhejiang University, Hangzhou, China. The work in this account concerns numerical methods for solid and porous-media mechanics and their combination with data-driven solvers.

## Research Interests

- Numerical Manifold Method (NMM) and partition-of-unity based discretizations
- Coupled hydro-mechanical problems: static and dynamic Biot consolidation
- Stability of mixed formulations under the inf-sup condition
- Physics-informed neural networks and weak-form learning
